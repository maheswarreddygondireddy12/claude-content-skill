# Generic visual system

The default tokens are a starting point, not a required identity. Change the palette, type pairing, motion personality, safe zones, and audio targets to fit the project and platform. Keep choices consistent within one video while allowing each project to establish its own look.

- The opening hook and story take priority over a logo or title card.
- Use one main accent at a time; add another only when it communicates a comparison or state.
- Keep essential text away from platform controls and preserve readable contrast.
- Let the user's voice-over or dialogue lead. Music and sound effects should support it.
- Select a font that supports every language and script used in the captions.
- Use `tokens.json` for canvas, color, typography, safe-area, motion, and audio defaults. These values are editable defaults, not universal platform specifications.
