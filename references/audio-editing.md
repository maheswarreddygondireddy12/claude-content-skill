# Audio editing

- Treat dialogue or voice-over as the anchor. Confirm sync before detailed sound design.
- Remove distracting noise carefully; avoid artifacts from aggressive denoising.
- Use EQ and compression only to solve audible problems. Preserve natural dynamics where they support delivery.
- Keep music below speech and automate it around important words. Ducking should sound smooth, not pump visibly.
- Place sound effects to support actions, reveals, or transitions. Reduce or omit any cue that competes with speech.
- Check peaks and integrated loudness. The supplied QC tool targets approximately −14 LUFS for normalized social video audio, but platform requirements and source needs vary.
- Review the mix on headphones and small speakers. Check for clipping, channel issues, silence, and intelligibility.

The mastering helper normalizes loudness and copies the video stream. It is not a substitute for balancing dialogue, music, and effects in the edit.
