# Color correction and grading

The included project supplies color tokens and renderer styling. It does not automatically analyze or grade arbitrary footage. Use a video editor for shot-level correction and grading.

1. Confirm color space, frame rate, and exposure interpretation for the source.
2. Correct exposure and white balance before applying a creative look.
3. Match adjacent shots for exposure, contrast, and skin tone before adding a show-wide look.
4. Use scopes when available; check highlights, shadows, and saturation for clipping.
5. Apply a restrained creative treatment that supports the story and remains consistent across shots.
6. Review on more than one display when possible and check the exported file.

Keep captions and graphics at strong contrast. Avoid relying on hue alone to communicate meaning.
